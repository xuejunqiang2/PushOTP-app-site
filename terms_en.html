2. Service scope

PUSH OTP provides standard one-time passwords, enterprise device binding, display of enterprise operation requests, approval or rejection, and verification records.

  • Standard one-time passwords are generated locally. We cannot recover an OTP secret that you have not backed up.
  • Enterprise verification requests originate from the relevant enterprise system. PUSH OTP displays the request and returns your decision; it does not perform the sign-in, transfer, or other enterprise operation.
  • An approval does not guarantee that an operation completes. The result depends on the enterprise system, network conditions, and enterprise rules.

3. Your responsibilities

  • Review the application, user, event, IP address, time, and description before approval;
  • Reject requests that you did not initiate, that contain incorrect information, or that appear suspicious, and contact the enterprise administrator;
  • Remove an old device binding when a device is replaced, lost, or no longer used.

7. Deletion and stopping use

PUSH OTP does not create a user account that requires registration or sign-in, so there is no PUSH OTP account to close. You may delete an individual authenticator entry or use Clear All Data. We cannot restore the secret after a standard OTP entry is deleted. For an enterprise authenticator, the app first requests removal of the server-side binding and deletes local data after that succeeds. Contact support if you need further deletion of server-side information.
